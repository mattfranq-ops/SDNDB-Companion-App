=====================================================
 SDNDBZ COMPANION : SETUP AND PLAY GUIDE
=====================================================

HOW SHARING WORKS NOW  (READ THIS FIRST)

The app used to sync through an online service. It does not anymore.

Now ONE person (the DM) hosts the table right on their own computer, and
everyone else connects to them. This is the same way Foundry works.

What that means:
 * The DM opens the app and starts hosting. Their computer IS the table.
 * Players open the app and join the DM's address.
 * The DM must keep the app OPEN for players to stay connected. If the DM
   closes it, the table goes offline until they reopen it.
 * Nothing is stored online. The whole world lives on the DM's machine.

You need one of these so players can reach the host:
 * Same house or same Wi Fi ....... nothing extra, just the host's local address.
 * Over the internet ............... a free VPN like Hamachi or ZeroTier (Part 4).


-----------------------------------------------------
PART 1 : INSTALLING  (EVERYONE)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Run "SDNDBZ Companion Setup 1.12.0.exe".
2. Choose where to install (or accept the default) and finish.
3. Launch "SDNDBZ Companion" from the Start Menu or the desktop icon.
4. On first launch you see a credits screen. Scroll to the bottom, then click
   "I Acknowledge".
5. Next you see two choices: "Host a table" and "Join a table". See below.


-----------------------------------------------------
PART 2 : FOR THE HOST  (THE DM)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. On the start screen choose "Host a table".
2. TABLE NAME : type any name, such as FRIEZA CREW. This is the room your
   players will join. Leave PORT as 30000 unless you have a reason to change it.
3. Click "Start Hosting".
4. The app shows one or more addresses, for example:
        192.168.1.20 : 30000     (your LAN address)
        25.x.x.x : 30000         (your Hamachi address, if Hamachi is running)
   Read the correct one to your players. Part 4 explains which to use.
5. Also give players the TABLE NAME. They need BOTH the address and the name.
6. Keep the app open while you play. Your world saves automatically on your PC.

Note: the host also plays in the same window. You are hosting AND joined at once,
so you do not need a second copy open.


-----------------------------------------------------
PART 3 : FOR PLAYERS
-----------------------------------------------------
1. On the start screen choose "Join a table".
2. HOST ADDRESS : type the address the DM gave you (for example 25.1.2.3).
3. PORT : 30000  (unless the DM told you a different number).
4. TABLE NAME : type the exact name the DM gave you.
5. Click "Connect".

Once connected you see the same live characters, NPCs, combat tracker, notes,
and map as everyone else. Changes appear for the whole table in real time.

If it says it cannot reach the table server:
 * The DM has not started hosting yet, or closed the app.
 * The address or table name has a typo.
 * You are not on the same network or VPN as the DM (see Part 4).


-----------------------------------------------------
PART 4 : PLAYING OVER THE INTERNET
-----------------------------------------------------
Everyone in the same house on the same Wi Fi:
 * Use the host's LAN address (usually starts with 192.168 or 10.). Done.

Players in different places:
 * Use a free VPN so everyone looks like they are on one network.
   1. Everyone installs Hamachi (or ZeroTier).
   2. The DM creates a network; players join that network.
   3. The DM reads their Hamachi address (it starts with 25.) to the players.
   4. Players type that 25.x.x.x address into HOST ADDRESS when they Join.

The app cannot open your home network to the internet by itself. That is what
the VPN handles, and why remote play needs one.


-----------------------------------------------------
PART 5 : A QUICK TOUR
-----------------------------------------------------
The bottom bar has five sections:
 * Players ...... build and run character sheets.
 * NPCs ......... statblocks and a bestiary for the DM.
 * Session ...... the live table: initiative tracker, dice rolls, Quick Combat,
                  Environment and hazards, a clock and calendar, and notes.
 * Universe ..... the galaxy map, planets, store, and economy.
 * Compendium ... rules data, tables, and Materoids Flavors (optional rules).

THE BATTLE MAP
 Click the "MAP" arrow on the right edge (works on Players, NPCs, or Session)
 to roll out the tactical map. Tokens come from the initiative tracker. Drag a
 token to move it, and each token has an altitude for flying. A fighter in a
 form takes that form's aura color, and giant forms like Great Ape grow the
 token.

OPTIONAL RULES
 Compendium : Materoids Flavors holds the optional systems. Turn on things like
 Harsh Galaxies (a planet's weather and hazards affect the fight) or Quick
 Combat (resolve a skirmish in one roll instead of playing it out).


-----------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
-----------------------------------------------------
 * Players cannot connect ...... check that the DM clicked "Start Hosting" and
                                 left the app open, that everyone typed the SAME
                                 table name, and that remote players are on the
                                 same Hamachi or ZeroTier network as the DM.
 * Firewall popup on the host .. allow the app through so players can reach it.
 * Nothing syncs ............... you are probably on different table names. They
                                 must match exactly, including spelling.
 * Where is my data ............ the world is saved on the DM's computer only.
                                 Treat the DM's machine as the source of truth.

Have fun.
